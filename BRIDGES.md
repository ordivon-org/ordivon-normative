# Cross-Owner Bridges

- **World**: generic identity, time, causation, realization, occurrence, succession/branching and external anchor facts.
- **Human**: intentions, beliefs, consent/assent as human states, reasons-responsiveness and cognitive/affective facts; only admitted normative status/consequence is Normative-owned.
- **Harness**: operational mediation may consume externally supplied constitutive/normative truth; Harness does not manufacture upstream validity, legitimacy, fairness or remedy correctness.
- **Host**: Host owns durable Task continuity and local operational integrity; HDF43 order-relative validity is an external bridge, not generic Host normative authority.
- **Finance**: economic/market/instrument/account/position/settlement facts remain Finance-owned; context-relative entitlement, obligation, permission, competence or validity semantics may be Normative-owned.
