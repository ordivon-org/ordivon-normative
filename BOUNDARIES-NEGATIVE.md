# Boundaries and Negative Results

Not owned here:

- universal moral, epistemic, aesthetic or prudential normativity;
- generic institutions/organizations/governance processes;
- political/legal Constitution as an institutional object;
- legitimacy simpliciter or effective power/control/enforcement;
- reason-object ontology and motivating/explanatory reasons;
- robust reasons/value/ought/fittingness/good-reasoning/normative-explanation as a single generic owner;
- generic Representation/conceptual-resource ownership;
- generic Control/Regulation ownership.

No ONF or PHR numbered Foundation series is admitted.
