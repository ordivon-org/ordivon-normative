# Foundation Architecture

Numbered Foundation Count = **0**.

The frozen foundation-level architecture is non-numbered:

- **AnchorInterface** — typed external anchors such as practice, adoption, actors, history, founding events, prior contexts and imported facts.
- **GeneratorAdmissionStructure** — plural roots, custom, precedent, recognition, authorization, supersession, inheritance, conflict, gaps and indeterminacy.
- **Constitutive/Normative Derivation-and-Transition Semantics** — context-relative status, consequence, deontic output, competence/power and valid transformation.

World supplies generic identity/lifecycle/causal facts through a typed bridge.
