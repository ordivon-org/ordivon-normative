# Owner Contract

Ordivon Normative owns typed anchor-to-generator admission and context-relative constitutive/deontic/competence/change semantics.

It may determine applicability, status, deontic consequence, explicit permission, priority/defeat/conflict, closure, normative transition, automatic trigger, amendment and competence/power queries inside an admitted context.

It does not absorb the ontologies of reason objects, human psychology, institutions, finance, physical capability, effective control, legitimacy simpliciter, or generic value/ought/fittingness.
